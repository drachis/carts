# Code notes

The Lua modules carry no comments; notes on them live here, by module and
function. Anything not noted works as it does in Wyrmfire, whose
`CODE_NOTES.md` covers the parsers, particles, breath, fury and legend rhythm.

## data.lua

- `ages`: a row's `stats` are added to the dragon by `grant`. `ram`, `guard`
  and `storm` start at 0 and are the three powers. Each age adds a little
  `sink` and takes a little `accel`; the two must never meet, or the dragon
  cannot lift off (it happened once).
- `growth`: what one full belly adds.
- `unit_specs`: `color` marks a legend. `grants` moves the dragon to its next
  age and ends the stage; `crown` wins the game. `size` is the size drawn, in
  pixels (16 is a 2x2 sprite). `aura` is the radius of a glow that is drawn
  around the unit and is what can be hit. `pool` on a spawn point is what it
  spawns; on a legend, what it summons.

## level.lua

Generated by `gen_cart.py`; do not edit. `spawn_text` rows are `name,x,y` in
pixels; a row belongs to the stage `y\128`.

## core.lua

- `update_particles`: an ember lights whatever flammable tile it is in, then
  dies if the tile is still solid. A coin with `meal` set is meat: it flies
  to the belly gauge, not the dragon, and feeds it on arrival.

## world.lua

- `blocked`: a 7x7 box (3 either side of the centre), smaller than a tile, so
  four corners are enough.
- `slide`: moves a pixel at a time on each axis and reports which axis hit.
  A body with `reach` is tested at both ends of its width.
- `ignite`: a lit tile becomes `tile_fire` at once and remembers what to
  leave behind; at `spread_at` frames left it lights its four neighbours.
- `ram`: breaks a brittle tile, or a stone one once the dragon has `ram`.
- `populate`: the units of the current stage only; relics of every stage.

## units.lua

- `update_units`: only units near the screen are updated. `cy` is the middle
  of what is drawn and `reach` half its width beyond a person's, so breath,
  charge and contact all measure to the edge of a big unit.
- `unit_ai.legend`: for the first 40 frames of each cycle it is `reeling`
  (still, a star overhead, triple fire). `windup` shows the second sprite
  (id + 8). Below half health its clock runs double.
- `unit_ai.spawner`: spawns while fewer than `horde_size` + 3 per age units
  are near the screen.
- `draw_units`: a `size` over 8 is drawn from a 16x16 sprite.

## dragon.lua

- `enter_stage`: the map's stages sit 128 pixels apart, so the camera's y is
  the stage.
- `update_dragon`: on the ground with no upward input the dragon is in ground
  mode: `run` times the acceleration, and the aim is kept level. After
  moving, it is held to the right of the screen's left edge; anything it is
  pushed into there is rammed, and if the screen is creeping it is hurt.
- `charge_impacts`: rams a strip as tall as the dragon's body.
- `draw_dragon`: on the ground the trunk is lifted to stand a leg's length
  over the floor line while the tail rests on it. Limbs are two lines with a
  knee: grounded they step and carry the body, in flight the leg trails and
  the arm reaches forward.
- `feed`: called when meat reaches the gauge.

## main.lua

- `update_camera`: follows at most 4 pixels a frame, never back. After
  `linger_frames` without progress it creeps. `advance` counts down the
  celebration after a legend falls, then starts the next stage.
- `_draw`: the hoard is drawn as a heap in the lair, three gold to a coin.
