## Title

Witchcap

## Short description (tagline)

A witch, a recipe, sixty seconds, and a forest full of mushrooms that all
look the same.

## Description

**Witchcap** is a night-time scavenger hunt for the PICO-8.

You are a witch with a potion to brew. The recipe is pinned along the
bottom of the screen and the cauldron is waiting in the clearing. Run out
into the swamp, find the mushrooms the recipe asks for, and bring them
back before the moon wanes to nothing.

The trouble is the lookalikes. Every mushroom comes in four forms that
differ by a pixel or two: a spot in a different place, a ring on the stem,
one dimple more. There is no "good" form to memorise. Each run the recipe
names its own, so you have to look, every time.

Pick the wrong one and you are poisoned: slow, green, grounded, with the
whole forest dancing faster while you try to read it.

There are no enemies. The only thing chasing you is the clock, and the
only thing that can hurt you is your own hurry.

And you are not alone out there. Baba Yaga's hut, taller than the screen,
lumbers after you on its chicken legs the whole way.

### Features

- **Nine levels.** The first asks for one mushroom in a forest of
  strangers. The last asks for five among seventeen imposters.
- **A new forest every run**, wrapped into a ring: keep walking and you
  come back round to the cauldron.
- **Sixteen mushrooms** in four families, all of them dancing.
- **A broom** that carries you a screen's width in one swoop, then needs
  a rest.
- **The moon is your clock.** Watch it wane.
- **Eight potions** to brew, each with its own colour, revealed when the
  pot boils over.
- **Par times and personal bests** for every level.
- **A cackle button**, because you are a witch.

### Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| Left / Right | Run |
| O (Z or C) | Jump. Hold for a higher jump |
| X | Pick the mushroom you are standing at |
| Up | Fly the broom |
| Down | Cackle |

On the title screen and after a failed brew, Left and Right choose a
level from those you have unlocked.

### How to play

1. Read the recipe at the bottom of the screen.
2. Find each mushroom on it. Check it against the recipe before you pick.
3. Stand at the cauldron to empty your basket into the pot. The little
   cauldron in the status bar points the way home.
4. Get the last mushroom into the pot before the moon is gone.
