# Code notes

The Lua modules carry no comments; notes on them live here, by module and
function. Anything not noted works as it does in Wyrmfire, whose
`CODE_NOTES.md` covers the parsers, particles, breath, fury and legend rhythm.

## data.lua

- `ages`: a row's `stats` are added to the dragon by `grant`. Each age adds a
  little `sink` and takes a little `accel`; the two must never meet, or the
  dragon cannot lift off (it happened once).
- `growth`: what one full belly adds. Each growth costs `growth_rate` times
  the meat of the last (`belly_goal`), and `max_size` caps the dragon.
- Scale: enemy health and the dragon's damage are sized so that King Arthur
  has 128 health, and 1 health means "dies to any hit". Keep any health
  times 127 under 32767, or divide first: a bar once overflowed.
- `unit_specs`: `color` marks a legend. `grants` moves the dragon to its next
  age and ends the stage; `crown` wins the game. `size` is the size drawn, in
  pixels (16 is a 2x2 sprite). `aura` is the radius of a glow that is drawn
  around the unit and is what can be hit. `pool` on a spawn point is what it
  spawns; on a legend, what it summons. `shield` is its bubbles.
- `tiles` and `placed` say what each character of a stamp is: a sprite id,
  or the name of a unit or relic.
- `stamps`: a building, as rows joined by `:`, the last row on the ground.
  Every row of a stamp is the same width. `.` is the back wall of an
  interior.
- `blocks`: the kinds of block, mildest first, each a list of stamps joined
  by `+`. Block `b` (1..3) of stage `s` (0..3) is kind `s+b`.
- `hordes`: what the director sends in each stage.

## level.lua

Generated by `gen_cart.py`; do not edit. Constants only: where the dragon
starts, the stages' names, tile ids.

## core.lua

- `update_particles`: fire rises harder the older it gets (`buoyancy`), so a
  jet goes out flat and billows up at the end. An ember lights whatever
  flammable tile it is in; against anything still solid it steps back and
  either licks up a wall or spreads along a floor, losing a little life.
  Embers are turned aside by a legend with bubbles. A coin with `meal` set
  is meat: it flies to the belly gauge and feeds the dragon on arrival.

## world.lua

- `blocked`: a 7x7 box (3 either side of the centre), smaller than a tile, so
  four corners are enough.
- `slide`: moves a pixel at a time on each axis and reports which axis hit.
  A body with `reach` is tested at both ends of its width.
- `standing`: a solid tile that can be destroyed at all (flags 2, 4 or 16).
- `unseat`: called when a solid tile goes. It counts toward `razed` and
  queues the tile above and the tiles either side in `falls`.
- `update_fire`: a queued fall, when its wait is up, brings the tile down if
  it is `standing` with nothing solid under it, which unseats its own
  neighbours in turn. That is the whole of collapse: towers pancake and
  roofs follow their walls.
- `ignite`: a lit tile becomes `tile_fire` at once (so it no longer holds
  anything up) and remembers what to leave behind; at `spread_at` frames
  left it lights its four neighbours.
- `ram`: breaks a brittle tile; a stone one only when `charging`.
- `build_block`: stamps random buildings of the block's kind along the
  ground from `block_from` to `block_to`, placing their units and relics,
  and stands the barrier at `block_to`. The gap between buildings widens as
  the dragon's health falls.

## units.lua

- `update_units`: only units near the screen are updated. `cy` is the middle
  of what is drawn and `reach` half its width beyond a person's, so breath,
  charge and contact all measure to the edge of a big unit.
- `damage_unit`: a unit whose `max_hp` is 1 dies to any damage.
- Shields: fire slides off a shielded unit and is thrown back. A stunned
  unit (`unit_ai.stunned`) stands under a star and takes triple fire; a
  legend's bubbles return when the stun ends, anyone else's are gone.
- `unit_ai.legend`: presses, winds up, lunges, on a 200-frame clock.
  `windup` shows the second sprite (id + 8). Below half health the clock
  runs double.
- `unit_ai.panic`: a burning unit runs for its burn time and then takes one
  hit.
- `draw_units`: a `size` over 8 is drawn from a 16x16 sprite.

## dragon.lua

- `next_block`: moves on to the next third of the stage, builds it, and
  counts what stands in it (`built`).
- `enter_stage`: the map's stages sit 128 pixels apart, so the camera's y is
  the stage.
- `update_dragon`: on the ground with no upward input the dragon is in ground
  mode: `run` times the acceleration, and the aim is kept level. Before it
  moves, whatever brittle tile its head or body is about to enter is rammed,
  which is how it flies through timber.
- The head is part of the body for walls: a move that would put the head in a
  solid tile is refused, unless the head is already in one (so turning can
  never trap it) or the dragon is charging.
- `charge_impacts`: rams, stone included, a strip as tall as the body and
  past the head. A unit with `shields` loses one and takes no bite; losing
  the last stuns it for `stagger_frames`.
- `damage_dragon`: while `guarding` (the guard earned, on the ground, no
  direction held, not breathing) every blow and shot is turned away.
- `draw_dragon`: on the ground the trunk stands a leg's length over the
  floor line while the tail rests on it. Limbs are two lines with a knee.
  The wing is a bat's: an arm to a wrist, four finger bones, and a scalloped
  membrane between each pair. `segment` draws one body circle with a spine.
- `update_tail`: no part of the body, for drawing or for being hit, goes
  below the ground line.
- `breathe_fire`: power is constant for the whole exhale and the breath ends
  at once with a cough of smoke; it shakes the screen while it lasts and has
  a bright core at the mouth. A flame's size barely depends on the dragon's.
- `feed`: called when meat reaches the gauge. Soldiers are worth almost
  nothing to the belly; livestock is what grows the dragon.

## main.lua

- `update_camera`: follows the dragon, and is also the director. `heat` is
  how far the block is toward `raze_goal`. At full heat the barrier at
  `block_to` is cleared and the next block built, or, in the last block, the
  stage's legend is spawned once. At `rally_at` a band of knights arrives
  once per block. `muster` counts down faster with heat;
  when it runs out and the crowd is under its limit, a band appears just off
  one edge of the screen and walks in. `advance` counts down the celebration
  after a legend falls, then starts the next stage.
- `_draw`: the hoard is drawn as mounds in the lair.
