# Code notes

The Lua modules carry no comments; notes on them live here, by module and
function. Anything not noted works as it does in Wyrmfire, whose
`CODE_NOTES.md` covers the parsers, particles, breath, fury and legend rhythm.

## data.lua

- `ages`: a row's `stats` are added to the dragon by `grant`. `charge_speed`
  starts at 0, which is what keeps a whelp from charging; `flaps` and
  `flight` likewise start at 0.
- `unit_specs`: `color` marks a legend (it gets the boss bar and is always
  eaten). `ward` is the sprite id of the seal that falls with it, `grants`
  moves the dragon to its next age, `crown` wins the game.
- `shot_specs.lob` is a flight time in frames; `shoot` solves for the
  velocity that lands on the dragon under the shot's `drop`.

## level.lua

Generated by `gen_cart.py`; do not edit. `spawn_text` rows are
`name,x,y` in pixels.

## core.lua

- `update_particles`: an ember (`p.ember`) lights whatever flammable tile it
  is in, then dies if the tile is still solid. Debris falls.

## world.lua

- `blocked`: every body is a 7x7 box (3 either side of its centre), smaller
  than a tile, so four corners are enough.
- `slide`: moves a pixel at a time on each axis and reports which axis hit.
- `sight`: eight samples along the line; used for the breath, and for what a
  unit can see.
- `ignite`: a lit tile becomes `tile_fire` at once, so burning wood no longer
  blocks; the fire remembers what to leave behind. At `spread_at` frames left
  it lights its four neighbours, which is what makes vines a fuse.
- `ram`: called by the charge on the tiles ahead of the dragon.
- `populate`: rebuilds every unit and relic from `spawn_text`, skipping
  legends in `slain` and relics in `taken` (keyed by their row).

## units.lua

- `update_units`: `unit.sees` is range plus `sight`. A walker that is stopped
  by a wall while it wants to move hops.
- `patrol`: turn at a wall or a ledge.
- `unit_ai.legend`: holds its clock until it first sees the dragon. Its lunge
  is along the ground.

## dragon.lua

- `update_dragon`: before flight, up never tilts the aim while moving
  sideways, so a running jump still breathes level; up alone aims straight
  up. A charge is level before flight and follows the aim after.
- `update_tail`: tail segments sag unless something solid is under them.
- `draw_dragon`: `ux,uy` is the body's "up", flipped so the wing is always on
  the sky side.
- `rest`: runs on entering a nest tile, not while standing in one.

## main.lua

- `_draw`: colour 12 belongs to the seal tiles alone, so remapping it around
  `map()` makes them shimmer. The sky is one rectangle over the top 29 rows.
- `draw_hud`: the minimap is 8x4 rooms at two pixels each.
